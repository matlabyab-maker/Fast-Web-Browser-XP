Fast Browser XP — Split Screen
================================
Feature specification:
- Split the main browser area into two independent panes.
- Each pane has its own WebView and address/navigation controls.
- Two different URLs can be opened simultaneously.
- A draggable divider separates the panes.
- SPLIT toggles split mode; CLOSE SPLIT returns to the normal single-page view.
- Both panes remain independently scrollable and navigable.
