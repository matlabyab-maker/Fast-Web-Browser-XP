Fast Browser XP Plus - Fullscreen and FLOAT update

- FULL button enters immersive fullscreen and hides Android status/navigation bars.
- F11 also toggles fullscreen on compatible keyboards.
- A floating WINDOW button appears over the page while fullscreen and exits back to browser window mode.
- FLOAT button enters Android Picture-in-Picture mode where supported; the system can move the browser as a floating window.
- Back exits fullscreen first, then normal browser navigation.
- .git is intentionally excluded from the ZIP.
